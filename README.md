# Kharcha

A simple expense tracker that runs in your browser. No sign-up, no server, no data included.

**Open it:** visit the GitHub Pages link for this repo, or download `index.html` and open it.

## What it does

- **Dashboard** – spent this month, everyday budget left, today's spending against a daily limit, a donut of spending by category and a bar graph of spending by day.
- **Add & list** – add what you spent, money you received, or transfers between your own accounts. Entries are grouped by day and can be deleted.
- **Plan** – your salary, savings target and fixed monthly items (rent, EMI, subscriptions…). Kharcha works out the everyday budget, a daily limit, and whether you're on track. You can add or remove categories.
- **Savings** – a separate savings balance with money added or taken out, and goals that fill up in order.

Use the ‹ › arrows to move between months.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter is saved only in the browser you use (local storage). Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is stored in this repository. Use **Export** to download a backup file and **Import** to load it on another device or browser.
